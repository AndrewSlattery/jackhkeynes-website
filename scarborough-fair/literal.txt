Are you going to the fair of Cherbourg?
Parsley, sage, rosemary and thyme,
Speak of me to my former love:
He who was once all mine.

Let him fashion me a shirt of sackcloth,
Parsley, sage, rosemary and thyme,
Without any thread, without stitch or seam;
He will be all mine again.

Let him provide me with an acre of fallow land,
Parsley, sage, rosemary and thyme,
Caught between the shore and the water of the sea;
He will be all mine again.

Let him reap it with a scythe of fern,
Parsley, sage, rosemary and thyme,
And bind the harvest with a sprig of heather;
He will be all mine again.

Are you going to the fair of Cherbourg?
Parsley, sage, rosemary and thyme,
Speak of me to my former love:
He who was once all mine.
