In the midst of the nights covering my skies,
the black abyss with no way out,
I bless whatever gods there may be
for the soul forever unconquered.

Caught in the vice of circumstance,
I have neither flinched nor cried out.
Under the scourge of ill luck
my bloodied brow is not bowed.

Far beyond this place of rage
there prowls in the shadow only the Horror,
but the menace of the long age
finds me, and will find me, without fear.

No matter the narrow threshold of death,
or the scroll laden with blame,
I am the master of my fate,
the captain of my soul.
