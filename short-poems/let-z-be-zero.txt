Let z be zero on the complex plane,
Add c to z-by-z, and that’s z-one,
Now iterate again and yet again;
The sequence that we make is never done,
But can at least be sorted by its fate:
To wander off or stay within the disc
In which the magnitudes of points equate
At most to two (for should a sequence risk
Those outer wilds but once, it must in time
Diverge forever, never to return).
The c whose iterates refuse to climb
Such heights shall be our primary concern.
The set of all the c that we have got
Deserves a name—let’s call it Mandelbrot.
