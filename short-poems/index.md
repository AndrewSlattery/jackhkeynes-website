## William McGonagall

{% capture p %}{% include_relative william-mcgonagall.txt %}{% endcapture %}
{% include poem.html text=p %}

Written on Discord, 31 August 2025. A
[double dactyl](https://www.poetryfoundation.org/education/glossary/double-dactyl)
on William McGonagall's
[*The Tay Bridge Disaster*](https://www.poetryfoundation.org/poems/45828/the-tay-bridge-disaster)
(1880), his poem on the
[collapse of the Tay Bridge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tay_Bridge_disaster)
at Dundee in December 1879. Its opening line, *Beautiful Railway Bridge of the
Silv'ry Tay!*, is a
[hendecasyllable](https://www.poetryfoundation.org/education/glossary/hendecasyllabic).
{: .poem-note}

## Ozymandias Redux

{% capture p %}{% include_relative ozymandias-redux.txt %}{% endcapture %}
{% include poem.html text=p %}

[Written on Tumblr](https://www.tumblr.com/jackhkeynes/821043559915552768/i-met-a-traveller-flying-business-class-who),
2 July 2026, in reply to a suggested sequel. After Percy Bysshe Shelley's
[*Ozymandias*](https://www.poetryfoundation.org/poems/46565/ozymandias) (1818),
following his rhyme scheme. Shelley wrote his
[sonnet](https://www.poetryfoundation.org/education/glossary/sonnet) in a
friendly contest with Horace Smith, whose own
[*Ozymandias*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ozymandias_(Smith)) appeared a
few weeks later.
