<div class="hb-flavour" markdown="1">

Stobor (pronounced STOH-boar) are mortals descended from awakened facsimiles of people: clay tomb sentinels raised by priest-kings, wooden puppets animated by fey tricksters, porcelain dolls infused with life. Over generations, the magic that animated those figures settled into true flesh and blood.

Stobor are born, age, and die like other humanoids, living for up to two centuries, but their bodies still bear the hallmarks of their crafted origins. Their skin might have the sheen of glazed ceramic or the faint grain of carved wood, and it wears over time to a polished finish, like a well-handled antique. Their bones and joints are impossibly precise, and they can hold unnervingly still for hours at a time.

Stobor communities are most often found in the oldest districts of large cities, in the trading quarters, workshops, and temple wards where their ancestors were once put to use.

## <span class="hb-tag">Trait</span> Maker's Mark

You have an instinct for crafted things. You gain proficiency with one type of Artisan's Tools of your choice and in one of the following skills of your choice: Arcana, History, or Investigation.

In addition, if you handle a crafted object for 1 minute, you learn roughly when it was made, which tradition of crafters made it, and whether it's an original or a copy.

## <span class="hb-tag">Trait</span> Stobor Lineage

You carry the magic of one of the three Stobor lineages: Faience, Marionette, or Ushabti. Choose one; each is described under Stobor Lineages. You gain your lineage's level 1 benefit now, and you learn its spells at character levels 3 and 5.

</div>

***Lineage Spells.*** You always have your lineage spells prepared. You can cast each one once without a spell slot, and you regain the ability to cast it in that way when you finish a Long Rest. You can also cast it using any spell slots you have of the appropriate level.

***Spellcasting Ability.*** When you choose your lineage, choose Intelligence, Wisdom, or Charisma. That is your spellcasting ability for spells you cast with this trait, and it also sets the DC of your Still Life trait.

</div>

<div class="hb-feature hb-feature--flow" markdown="1">

<div class="hb-lead" markdown="1">

## <span class="hb-tag">Trait</span> Uncanny Stillness

You can be as motionless as the figures your ancestors once were, and you refuse to be made that way by anything else. You gain the following benefits.

</div>

***Still Life.*** As a Bonus Action, you become perfectly, lifelessly still. While you remain so, any creature that isn't already aware you're alive sees you as an inanimate figure, such as a statue, a mannequin, or a doll.

- **Seeing Through It.** A creature that touches you discerns that you're alive. Otherwise, it must take the Study action to inspect you and succeed on an Intelligence (Investigation) check against a DC equal to 8 plus your Proficiency Bonus and your Stobor Lineage spellcasting ability modifier.
- **Ending It.** The effect ends if you move, speak, take an action, a Bonus Action, or a Reaction, or take damage.
- **Ambush.** If you roll Initiative while holding still and no enemy has discerned that you're alive, you have Advantage on the roll.

***Self-Possessed.*** You have Advantage on saving throws you make to avoid or end the Paralyzed, Petrified, or Restrained condition.

</div>

## <span class="hb-tag">Lineage</span> Faience

Stobor of the faience (pronounced fay-YONCE) lineage carry the pristine magic of glass figurines and porcelain dolls. Their features often have an uncanny perfection, with vivid color on their cheeks or faint lines resembling kintsugi.

***Level 1: Golden Seams.*** You know the *Mending* cantrip, and you can target yourself with it. When you do, you can expend up to a number of Hit Point Dice equal to your Proficiency Bonus, regaining Hit Points as you would by spending them during a Short Rest; your wounds close in seams of gold. Once you expend dice this way, you can't do so again until you finish a Long Rest.

**Level 3:** *Sanctuary*. **Level 5:** *Suggestion*.

</div>

<div class="hb-feature" markdown="1">

## <span class="hb-tag">Lineage</span> Marionette

Stobor of the marionette lineage carry the animating spark of wooden puppets and cloth mannequins. Subtle seams and lines often trace their joints, and their long, dexterous fingers reflect a history of performance.

***Level 1: Borrowed Strings.*** You know the *Mage Hand* cantrip. In addition, as a Bonus Action, you can tug on the strings of a willing creature you can see within 30 feet of you: that creature can take a Reaction to move up to half its Speed without provoking Opportunity Attacks. You can use this Bonus Action a number of times equal to your Proficiency Bonus, and you regain all expended uses when you finish a Long Rest.

**Level 3:** *Command*. **Level 5:** *Rope Trick*.

</div>

<div class="hb-feature" markdown="1">

## <span class="hb-tag">Lineage</span> Ushabti

Stobor of the ushabti (pronounced oo-SHAB-tee) lineage trace their ancestry to stone crypt sentinels, clay guardians, and tin soldiers. They tend toward solid, rigid builds, and protective glyphs or the faint outlines of ceremonial armor are often etched into their skin. Their ancestors were made to answer when called, and many still feel that summons: when someone beside them is threatened, an ushabti steps forward and says, "Here I am."

***Level 1: Here I Am.*** You know the *True Strike* cantrip. In addition, when an ally you can see within 5 feet of you takes damage from an attack roll, you can take a Reaction to answer in their place: the ally takes no damage from that attack, and you take the same amount instead. You can use this Reaction a number of times equal to your Proficiency Bonus, and you regain all expended uses when you finish a Long Rest.

**Level 3:** *Shield of Faith*. **Level 5:** *Enlarge/Reduce*.

</div>
