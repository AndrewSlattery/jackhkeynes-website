<div class="hb-flavour" markdown="1">

Stobor (STOH-boar) descend from awakened facsimiles of people — clay tomb sentinels, fey-animated puppets, porcelain dolls — whose animating magic has settled, over generations, into flesh and blood.

They are born, age, and die like other humanoids, living up to two centuries, but their skin keeps the sheen of glaze or the grain of wood, polishing with age like a well-handled antique. Most live in the oldest quarters of large cities, where their ancestors once served.

## <span class="hb-tag">Trait</span> Maker's Mark

You gain proficiency with one type of Artisan's Tools and in one of these skills: Arcana, History, or Investigation.

By handling a crafted object for 1 minute, you learn roughly when it was made, by which tradition of crafters, and whether it's an original or a copy.

## <span class="hb-tag">Trait</span> Stobor Lineage

Choose a lineage from the Stobor Lineages table. You gain its level 1 benefit now, and its spells at character levels 3 and 5.

</div>

***Lineage Spells.*** You always have these spells prepared. You can cast each once without a spell slot, regaining the ability when you finish a Long Rest, and you can also cast them using spell slots of the appropriate level.

***Spellcasting Ability.*** Choose Intelligence, Wisdom, or Charisma when you choose your lineage. It is your spellcasting ability for these spells and sets the DC of Still Life.

</div>

<div class="hb-lead" markdown="1">

## <span class="hb-tag">Trait</span> Uncanny Stillness

You have preternatural control over if or when you move:

</div>

***Still Life.*** As a Bonus Action, you become perfectly still. Until you move, speak, take an action, Bonus Action, or Reaction, or take damage, any creature not already aware you're alive sees you as an inanimate figure, such as a statue or doll. A creature that touches you discerns the truth; otherwise it must take the Study action and succeed on an Intelligence (Investigation) check against DC 8 + your Proficiency Bonus + your lineage spellcasting ability modifier. If you roll Initiative while still and no enemy has discerned you, you have Advantage on the roll.

***Self-Possessed.*** You have Advantage on saving throws to avoid or end the Paralyzed, Petrified, or Restrained condition.

</div>

## <span class="hb-tag">Lineage</span> Faience

Faience (fay-YONCE) Stobor descend from glass figurines and porcelain dolls. They have an uncanny perfection, with vivid color on their cheeks or faint lines like kintsugi.

***Golden Seams.*** You know *Mending* and can target yourself with it. When you do, you can spend up to a number of Hit Point Dice equal to your Proficiency Bonus, regaining Hit Points as on a Short Rest as your wounds close in seams of gold. Once you do so, you can't again until you finish a Long Rest.

</div>

<div class="hb-feature" markdown="1">

## <span class="hb-tag">Lineage</span> Marionette

Marionette Stobor descend from wooden puppets and cloth mannequins. They have fine seams tracing their joints and long, dexterous fingers made for performance.

***Borrowed Strings.*** You know *Mage Hand*. As a Bonus Action, you can tug the strings of a willing creature you can see within 30 feet: it can take a Reaction to move up to half its Speed without provoking Opportunity Attacks. You can do this a number of times equal to your Proficiency Bonus, regaining all uses when you finish a Long Rest.

</div>

<div class="hb-feature" markdown="1">

## <span class="hb-tag">Lineage</span> Ushabti

Ushabti (oo-SHAB-tee) Stobor descend from crypt sentinels, clay guardians, and tin soldiers. They are rigidly built, often etched with protective glyphs or the outlines of ceremonial armor. Made to answer when called, they step forward when an ally is threatened.

***Here I Am.*** You know *True Strike*. When an ally you can see within 5 feet of you takes damage from an attack roll, you can take a Reaction to take that damage instead. You can do this a number of times equal to your Proficiency Bonus, regaining all uses when you finish a Long Rest.

</div>
