--- layout: default --- {%- comment -%} Layout for the D&D homebrew pages (dnd/homebrew/*.md). The .hb-columns flow lives here rather than in each page's markdown, because the title block is part of it: kicker, title and tagline sit at the head of the first column rather than in a band of their own across the page. The page files therefore contain only .hb-flavour and .hb-feature blocks, which become direct children of the flow (as .hb-feature--wide must be, to span it). A page holding a table too wide for one column can divide itself into two side-by-side flows by putting in its markdown at the dividing point: a spanning table then spans only its own half of the page. Pages without the marker render exactly one flow, as before. See .hb-spread in _sass/_homebrew.scss for what the split buys and what it costs. This is _layouts/page.html without the parts a standalone rules page has nothing to put in: the date line (pages have no date, so page.html renders an empty ), the puzzle embed, and the divider and prev/next nav. {%- endcomment -%} {%- assign hb_parts = content | split: '' -%}
